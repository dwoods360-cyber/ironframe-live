**A completed vendor questionnaire is not continuous third-party assurance.**

It answers: “What did they attest on a form, on a date?”  
It does not answer: “What is true about that dependency this month—and can we prove it?”

High-blast-radius vendors move faster than annual PDF cycles. When the only artifact is a scored spreadsheet, three questions go unanswered:

• Which obligations are evidenced vs merely attested?  
• When was each critical vendor control last validated?  
• Who can authorize using that vendor evidence in a board or exam pack?

A practical control test this week:

1. Pick one critical or important ICT/third-party dependency.  
2. Pull the newest assurance artifact (not the newest questionnaire score).  
3. Trace source, date, scope, and who approved promoting it into your report.  
4. If the trail ends at “they filled out our form,” you have collection—not continuous assurance.

Control-first pattern for third-party evidence:

• Treat vendor packets as untrusted ingest until validated  
• Bind source, date, and scope before trusted use  
• Human-authorize promotion into exam or board exports  

Not a longer questionnaire. A shorter path to current, scoped proof.

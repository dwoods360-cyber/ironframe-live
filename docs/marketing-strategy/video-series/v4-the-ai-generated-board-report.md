# V4 — The AI-Generated Board Report (The Draft)

**Title card:**
When Risk Enters the Room
Episode 4: The Draft

**Length:** 75–90 seconds

## Scene 1 — Late evening

A risk analyst is preparing a board report.

The deadline is approaching.

An AI assistant produces a polished paragraph:

> The organization has fully remediated all material access-control deficiencies.

The analyst pauses.

One remediation item on the screen is still overdue.

## Scene 2 — The danger

The cursor moves toward **Send**.

**Narrator:**
“AI can produce confident language faster than an organization can verify it.”

## Scene 3 — Ironframe review gate

Inside Ironframe, the generated paragraph is marked:

> AI-assisted draft — not approved.

The system displays:

* supporting evidence;
* unresolved exceptions;
* source citations;
* reviewer assignment;
* required approval;
* publication status.

The analyst corrects the paragraph:

> Most access-control deficiencies have been remediated. One material exception remains open and is scheduled for review.

The general counsel approves the final wording.

## Scene 4 — Closing

The board packet is released only after approval.

**Narrator:**
“Ironframe allows AI to assist with analysis and drafting without allowing generated language to become an authorized statement by accident.”

**End card:**
IRONFRAME
AI assistance inside accountable governance.
Drafts never approve themselves.

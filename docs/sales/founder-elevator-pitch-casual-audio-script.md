## Script

### [0:00] Why this recording exists

This is your casual Ironframe pitch practice track. Same ideas as the commercial kit. Softer words. Use this when you are talking peer to peer and catalog language would sound salesy.

Pause two seconds.

source-file: docs/sales/founder-elevator-pitch-audio-script.md

### [0:20] The plain spine

Memorize this plain spine. Ironframe helps regulated companies see cyber and compliance risk in real dollars, with each client kept in its own locked workspace — not color charts and mixed evidence.

Pause two seconds.

### [0:45] Ten-second hallway

In ten seconds, say this. We help regulated companies and M S S Ps see cyber risk in real dollars, with each client in its own locked workspace — so you are not stuck with heatmaps or mixed client data.

Pause two seconds.

### [1:05] Thirty-second coffee version

In thirty seconds, say this. A lot of tools help you get a compliance badge fast. That is useful when sales needs a checkbox. We are for the other problem. Outside signals get cleaned before they land. Each client or subsidiary stays walled off. Risk shows up in dollars and cents, not red yellow green. You get a workspace you can actually run, and exports you can put in front of a board or auditor. If you run many clients, or a few subsidiaries, you get clean walls — not one shared folder pretending to be isolation.

Pause two seconds.

### [2:10] M S S P in plain words

If someone asks what you do for an M S S P, say this. Each client gets its own locked workspace. You track their controls there. You can show residual risk in dollars. You can export evidence without dumping every client into one pile. Mapping frameworks matters. Keeping clients separated and showing real money risk is usually why they care.

Pause two seconds.

### [2:50] Who else

You are not only talking to M S S Ps. Holding companies, portfolio operators, and regulated mid-market teams show up too — anyone who needs board-defendable exposure. If they only want a badge fast, that is a different product. Point them there. Do not force-fit.

Pause one second.

### [3:10] Versus Vanta — casual

When someone says, so you are like Vanta, keep it light. Those tools are great when you need a badge fast. We are for when the board asks what you are actually exposed for — and you cannot mix client evidence.

Pause two seconds.

### [3:30] Where is the A I — casual pocket

If someone asks where the A I is, say this. Not a chatbot guessing liability. We use governed agents behind a strict gateway to pull work forward, do the risk math in whole cents, and draft exports — with a human still signing off.

Pause two seconds.

### [4:00] Four loops — plain

If jargon gets in the way, remember four plain loops. Clean what comes in. Keep each client walled off. Turn threats into dollar exposure. Export proof scoped to that client.

Pause two seconds.

### [4:25] Soft ask — then price later

For informal rooms, ask soft first. If one of those pains is real — mixed clients, board dollars, or exportable evidence — we do a short workflow review on that pain. Not a demo tour. Only after they lean in, you can say the paid Design Partner seat is four thousand nine hundred ninety-nine dollars for a defined window — convert or exit.

Pause two seconds.

### [5:00] Close — practice cue

Practice the soft version out loud. Plain spine. Ten second hallway. Coffee elevator. M S S P in plain words. Casual Vanta contrast. Soft A I answer. Four plain loops. Soft ask first, price second. Switch to the commercial kit when the room wants sharper language.

Pause one second.

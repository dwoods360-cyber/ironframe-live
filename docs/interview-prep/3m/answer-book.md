## How to use

Do not memorize every answer word-for-word. Know:

- the opening point  
- the project or example you will use  
- three things you personally did  
- the result or lesson  
- how it relates to the 3M position  

Primary answers: **60–90 seconds**.  
“How did you do that?” → go deeper.  
Hostile follow-up → use tougher answer from prep notes.

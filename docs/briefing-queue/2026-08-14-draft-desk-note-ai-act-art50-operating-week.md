> **Signal (14 August 2026):** Article **50** transparency duties under the EU Artificial Intelligence Act have been applicable since **2 August 2026**. Mid-month is the first full operating week after applicability—when “we will get to AI Act later” stops being a calendar statement and becomes an evidence gap for chatbot disclosure, synthetic-content marking, and deployer deepfake / public-interest text labeling.
>
> *Do not treat “high-risk deferred” narratives as a pause on Article 50. Inventory role (provider vs deployer) per system.*

## What moved

National competent authorities can now treat chatbot disclosure, synthetic-content marking (subject to the limited legacy transition commonly described through **2 December 2026**), and deployer deepfake / public-interest text labeling as present-tense controls. The governance failure mode this week is category error: teams that only tracked deferred Annex III high-risk dates may lack provider/deployer inventories for Article 50 systems already in production.

Commission guidelines on Article 50 transparency remain the practical reading aid; they do not replace the regulation text.

## Governance implication (one test)

1. List production AI systems that chat with people, generate media/text, or perform emotion/biometric categorisation.  
2. Label each as provider, deployer, or both.  
3. For each, record the disclosure/marking control owner and evidence location (UI notice, watermarking/detection method, editorial-exception log)—or an explicit out-of-scope rationale.

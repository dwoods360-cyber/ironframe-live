[0:00] WHY THIS RECORDING EXISTS
--------------------------------
This is your casual Ironframe pitch practice track. Same ideas as the commercial kit.
Softer words. Use this when you are talking peer to peer and catalog language would
sound salesy.


[0:20] THE PLAIN SPINE
---------------------
Memorize this plain spine.

Ironframe helps regulated companies see cyber and compliance risk in real dollars, with
each client kept in its own locked workspace — not color charts and mixed evidence.


[0:45] TEN-SECOND HALLWAY
------------------------
In ten seconds, say this.

We help regulated companies and MSSPs see cyber risk in real dollars, with each client
in its own locked workspace — so you are not stuck with heatmaps or mixed client data.


[1:05] THIRTY-SECOND COFFEE VERSION
-----------------------------------
In thirty seconds, say this.

A lot of tools help you get a compliance badge fast. That is useful when sales needs a
checkbox. We are for the other problem. Outside signals get cleaned before they land.
Each client or subsidiary stays walled off. Risk shows up in dollars and cents, not red
yellow green. You get a workspace you can actually run, and exports you can put in front
of a board or auditor. If you run many clients, or a few subsidiaries, you get clean
walls — not one shared folder pretending to be isolation.


[2:10] MSSP IN PLAIN WORDS
--------------------------
If someone asks what you do for an MSSP, say this.

Each client gets its own locked workspace. You track their controls there. You can show
residual risk in dollars. You can export evidence without dumping every client into one
pile. Mapping frameworks matters. Keeping clients separated and showing real money risk
is usually why they care.


[2:50] WHO ELSE
---------------
You are not only talking to MSSPs. Holding companies, portfolio operators, and regulated
mid-market teams show up too — anyone who needs board-defendable exposure. If they only
want a badge fast, that is a different product. Point them there. Do not force-fit.


[3:10] VERSUS VANTA — CASUAL
----------------------------
When someone says, so you are like Vanta, keep it light.

Those tools are great when you need a badge fast. We are for when the board asks what
you are actually exposed for — and you cannot mix client evidence.


[3:30] WHERE IS THE AI — CASUAL POCKET
--------------------------------------
If someone asks where the AI is, say this.

Not a chatbot guessing liability. We use governed agents behind a strict gateway to pull
work forward, do the risk math in whole cents, and draft exports — with a human still
signing off.


[4:00] FOUR LOOPS — PLAIN
-------------------------
If jargon gets in the way, remember four plain loops.

1. Clean what comes in.
2. Keep each client walled off.
3. Turn threats into dollar exposure.
4. Export proof scoped to that client.


[4:25] SOFT ASK — THEN PRICE LATER
----------------------------------
For informal rooms, ask soft first.

If one of those pains is real — mixed clients, board dollars, or exportable evidence —
we do a short workflow review on that pain. Not a demo tour. Only after they lean in,
you can say the paid Design Partner seat is four thousand nine hundred ninety-nine
dollars for a defined window — convert or exit.


[5:00] CLOSE — PRACTICE CUE
---------------------------
Practice the soft version out loud. Plain spine. Ten second hallway. Coffee elevator.
MSSP in plain words. Casual Vanta contrast. Soft AI answer. Four plain loops. Soft ask
first, price second. Switch to the commercial kit when the room wants sharper language.

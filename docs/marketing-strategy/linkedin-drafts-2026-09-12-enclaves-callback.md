Connector count is still sold as maturity.

It answers a collection question. It does not answer whether one legal entity’s evidence can bleed into another client’s export from the same cabinet.

Soft tags and a shared login feel convenient. For MSSPs and multi-entity operators, they are not an assurance boundary. When an examiner for one entity can reach sister-entity evidence through the same console, scope expands—whether or not anyone intended a “shared pane.”

A Friday control test:

1. Name the legal entity the evidence pack is supposed to cover.
2. Ask who can authorize promoting that evidence outside the enclave.
3. Ask whether a second entity’s artifacts are reachable without a governed handoff.
4. If the answer is “we filter by tag,” you have tagging—not isolation.

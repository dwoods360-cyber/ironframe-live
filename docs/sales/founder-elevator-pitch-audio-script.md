## Script

### [0:00] Why this recording exists

This is your Ironframe founder pitch practice track. Listen until the lines feel natural. Then say them in your own words. This recording is for you — not for prospects on a workflow review call.

Pause two seconds.

source-file: docs/sales/market-entrance-playbook.md

### [0:20] The one-sentence spine

Memorize this spine. Ironframe is an invite-only quantitative G R C command post for regulated operators who have outgrown spreadsheets and refuse to manage liability with color-coded heatmaps.

Pause two seconds.

### [0:45] Ten-second hallway version

In ten seconds, say this. We give regulated operators and M S S Ps a multi-tenant command post — turning cyber risk into exact dollar exposure inside hard-isolated workspaces. No heatmaps, no mixed client data.

Pause two seconds.

### [1:05] Thirty-second elevator

In thirty seconds, say this. Most G R C tools optimize for speed to certification — for example S O C 2 in ninety days with hundreds of integrations. Ironframe is different. We are a command post for operations G R C. External intel is sanitized before it is saved. Each client or subsidiary lives in a hard isolated enclave. Risk is calculated in real dollars and cents — not red, yellow, green. Operators get a governed workspace and analyst exports they can put in a board pack. If you are an M S S P with many clients, or a holding company with a few subsidiaries, you get clean walls and exportable proof — not a shared folder with a compliance U I.

Pause two seconds.

### [2:10] Expand your M S S P answer

If someone asks what Ironframe does for an M S S P, do not stop at policy tracking. Say this. For an M S S P with multiple clients, Ironframe gives each client a sovereign workspace. You track controls and governance mapping per enclave, quantify residual risk in board-ready dollars, and export analyst-grade evidence — without a shared compliance swamp. Policy and framework mapping is part of it. Isolation and dollar risk are why they buy.

Pause two seconds.

### [2:50] Who else you sell to

You are not only M S S P. Also use holding companies and portfolio operators with a few subsidiaries, and regulated mid-market buyers who need board-defendable exposure. If they only want a compliance badge fast, that is another product category. Walk or complement — do not force-fit.

Pause one second.

### [3:10] Versus Vanta and Drata

When someone says, so you are like Vanta, answer with one contrast. They sell audit speed to certification. We sell financial risk defensibility and tenant sovereignty. Compliance automation is the floor, not the ceiling.

Pause two seconds.

### [3:30] Where is the A I — pocket answer

If someone asks where the A I is, do not lead with chat. Say this. We do not use A I as a chat box that hallucinates liability. We use governed A I agents behind a strict zero-trust gateway to automate ingestion, calculate deterministic risk math in whole cents, and draft exports — always with human-in-the-loop sign-off.

Pause two seconds.

### [4:00] Four loops to remember

If you forget agent names, remember four loops. Ingress — outside signals are sanitized before they land. Isolation — each client or entity is a hard wall. Quantification — threats become dollar exposure in whole cents. Export — tenant-scoped analyst files for board or audit.

Pause two seconds.

### [4:30] Price and ask

Commercial line for now. Paid Command Design Partner at four thousand nine hundred ninety-nine dollars for a defined co-builder window — convert or exit. Planned annual Command is about thirty-five thousand dollars per year — say planned until commercial G A. Your ask is not book a demo. Your ask is a short workflow review on one real pain — multi-client isolation, board dollar exposure, or exportable evidence.

Pause two seconds.

### [5:05] Close — practice cue

Practice out loud. One sentence spine. Ten second hallway. Thirty second elevator. M S S P expansion. Vanta contrast. A I pocket answer. Four loops. Price and workflow review ask. When those pieces are automatic, you can sell Ironframe in any hallway conversation.

Pause one second.

# V1 — The Risk Register (The Number)

**Title card:**
When Risk Enters the Room
Episode 1: The Number

**Length:** 75–90 seconds

## Scene 1 — Executive meeting

A boardroom screen shows a risk register filled with red, yellow, and green boxes.

The CFO leans forward.

**CFO:**
“What does ‘High’ mean in dollars?”

Silence.

The CISO glances at the risk manager. The risk manager scrolls through several spreadsheets.

**Narrator:**
“Many organizations can rank cyber risk. Far fewer can explain what the ranking means financially.”

## Scene 2 — The problem

Close shots:

* conflicting spreadsheet versions;
* an undocumented probability field;
* a loss estimate copied into an email;
* a board report marked “High Risk.”

**Narrator:**
“A color can support prioritization. But budgeting, insurance, materiality, and capital decisions require assumptions that can be examined.”

## Scene 3 — Ironframe

The Ironframe risk scenario opens.

The screen shows:

* scenario owner;
* event frequency assumptions;
* loss categories;
* evidence sources;
* uncertainty range;
* review history;
* financial exposure stored in whole cents.

**Narrator:**
“Ironframe connects the risk scenario to its assumptions, evidence, owners, controls, and approval history.”

The CFO clicks into the estimate and sees how it was constructed.

**CFO:**
“Now I can challenge the number.”

## Scene 4 — Closing

The original heatmap remains visible beside the quantified scenario.

**Narrator:**
“Ironframe does not make uncertainty disappear. It makes the reasoning visible.”

> **Signal (20 August 2026):** Phase II of CMMC was suspended on **13 July 2026**. Phase I self-assessment duties and **DFARS 252.204-7012** did not take the day off. If your mid-August plan was "wait for November certification theater," rewrite the plan.
>
> *Companion briefing: CMMC Phase II Pause Is Not DFARS Relief (queue draft dated 2026-08-20).*

## What moved

Public DoW notices hold the November 2026 Phase II transition. They also keep Phase I self-assessment language in force. Counsel alerts converge on the same operator point: **7012 safeguarding and incident reporting remain live** while third-party assessment cadence is reviewed. [1][2][3]

## Governance implication (one test)

1. Write one paragraph: what the Phase II pause does **not** cancel.
2. List contracts that still flow down 7012 regardless of CMMC assessment status.
3. Ask whether defense-client CUI evidence can bleed into a commercial client's shared ticket or GRC store without a governed export.

## Do not claim

- A live "90-day countdown" to mandatory Phase II on 10 Nov 2026
- That the pause waived NIST SP 800-171 or DFARS 7012
- Universal "primes rejecting SPRS" without named evidence
- Product pitch

Most programs can still color a risk red, amber, or green.

Fewer can answer the decision question in dollars: what is the estimated exposure for this scenario—and which assumptions produced that number?

Heatmaps remain useful as context. They become theater when they are the only decision layer: no quantified exposure, no visible methodology, no path from control evidence to a figure leadership can challenge.

A practical re-test for September:

1. Pick one “High” on the heatmap you already trust.
2. Ask for estimated exposure in whole cents (or say you cannot defend a number yet).
3. Ask which evidence and owner support that estimate.
4. If the trail ends at a color tile, that is the workflow to redesign—not another scoring workshop.

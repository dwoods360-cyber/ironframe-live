## Shortlist (spend ≤3 Audible/print credits here)

### 1. *The Tech Contracts Handbook* — David W. Tollen (ABA, 3rd ed.)

- **Why:** Clause-by-clause cloud/SaaS literacy for businesspeople — liability caps, indemnities, data/security terms, SLAs, clickwrap/sign-in-wrap.
- **Maps to:** `02-msa-terms-framework` · order-form incorporation · liability / indemnity ask in counsel memo.
- **How to use:** Skim indemnity + LoL + data chapters; mark questions in the counsel cover memo — do not redline live `/terms` from the book alone.
- **Format:** Print/ebook (ABA / Amazon). **Not on Audible** as of 2026-08 — spend an Audible credit elsewhere; buy this in print if you buy one legal book.

### 2. Privacy / DPA literacy (pick one US-first product book)

- **Why:** Controller/processor roles, DPA anatomy, breach clocks, subprocessor flow-downs — so you can review `03` / `04` / `05` intelligently.
- **Maps to:** `03-privacy-framework` · `04-dpa-framework` · `05-corporate-subprocessor-list` · `06-data-residency-statement`.
- **Search Audible/Amazon for:** “privacy law for startups,” “GDPR for product managers,” or a current **DPA / data processing** practitioner guide (US-first; EU chapters as awareness only until you sell there).
- **How to use:** Build a one-page checklist: roles, instructions, subprocessor notice, deletion/return, audit rights — then ask counsel to confirm Ironframe’s Path B stance.

### 3. Tech / SaaS contract negotiation playbook

- **Why:** What to give vs hold on redlines (caps, indemnities, SLAs, auto-renew, data ownership) during design-partner and later GA deals.
- **Maps to:** Path B convert-or-exit commercials · future customer paper negotiation · pairs with [message-constitution operator moves](../sales-enablement/message-constitution.md) (negotiate convert-or-exit — not free pilots).
- **Search for:** “negotiating technology contracts,” “SaaS contract negotiation,” or Guth-style SaaS negotiation handbooks if available on Audible.
- **How to use:** Write your **hold list** (e.g. no unlimited liability; Path B fee non-refundable; no free pilot) before the next prospect redline.

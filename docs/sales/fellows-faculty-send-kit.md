## If they say yes

1. Thank them · confirm **optional** + **capped seats** (state the number).  
2. Ask how they prefer students get the link (they share vs you share a short blurb).  
3. After 1–2 students run: request 10 min friction notes.  
4. Only then soft-open Track A student messages — still no Path B.

## If they say maybe / send more

- Point again at `/faculty-brief` and `/fellows#audits`.  
- Offer a 10-minute walkthrough of Mission 03 only (boundary) — no product tour circus.

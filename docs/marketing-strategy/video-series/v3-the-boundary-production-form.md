# IRONFRAME VIDEO 03 — THE BOUNDARY

**Format:** 75–90 second executive micro-story  
**Style:** Cinematic, restrained, credible, modern enterprise  
**Audience:** CISO, MSP / shared-services lead, Internal Audit, Privacy / DPO, Enterprise architect, Board risk committee  
**Core idea:** Multi-client and multi-entity systems are not safe because of labels. Isolation must hold across query, export, jobs, support actions, and admin privilege — and it must be testable.  
**Closing line:** Separation is not established by saying clients are separate. It is established by controls that can be tested.  
**CTA:** Request a 10–15 minute workflow review → ironframegrc.com/register/contact  

**Series title card:** When Risk Enters the Room · Episode 3: The Boundary  

**Locked stills (ChatGPT / refs → Flow Frames to Video):**  
`REF-ANALYST` · `REF-AUDITOR` · `REF-MSP-DESK` · `REF-SHARED-UI` · `REF-UI-BOUNDARY` · `REF-ISOLATION-REPORT`

**Anti-hallucination:** No SOC 2 / ISO claims · no invented customers · no Medshield / Vaultbank / Gridcore · fictional MSP **Harborline Managed** only · Client A / Client B labels OK · CTA = workflow review only

## SCENE 1 — THE WRONG CLIENT

**Time:** 0:00–0:23  

### Visual

MSP / shared-services desk. Dual monitors. Soft daylight. Muted navy and charcoal.

An analyst prepares a report for **Client A**. The report loads.

For a fraction of a second, a control record belonging to **Client B** appears in the results.

The analyst freezes.

### Dialogue — Analyst

"That should not be here."

### Sound

Quiet open-plan ambience. One wrong digital click on the flash. Hold silence after the line.

### Camera

Slow push to analyst → hard cut to screen flash → medium close freeze.

### On-screen text

*(optional, late in beat)*  
**Client A ≠ Client B**

## SCENE 2 — MORE THAN LABELS

**Time:** 0:23–0:42  

### Visual

Shared platform serving several clients and subsidiaries — generic console, not Ironframe.

Montage inserts:

* Export / download that ignores an entity filter  
* Abstract "view as client" / support privilege toggle  

### Voiceover

"Multi-client and multi-entity systems depend on more than labels."

Beat.

"Every query, export, background job, support action, and administrative privilege must remain inside its authorized boundary."

### Camera

Slow push on shared UI; hard inserts for export / privilege beats.

### On-screen text

**Boundary ≠ label**

## SCENE 3 — GOVERNED ISOLATION

**Time:** 0:42–0:57  

### Visual

Dark, clean Ironframe ops console (locked Harborline Boundary still).

Visible structure:

* Workspace identity  
* User role  
* Row-level policy  
* Entity scope  
* Query scope  
* Export restriction  
* Privileged action log  

A cross-tenant request is **denied**. An alert records the attempt.

### Voiceover

"Ironframe applies governed isolation throughout the workflow—not only at the interface."

### Camera

Gentle pan down the stack → deny toast → calm log entry.

### On-screen text

**Denied. Logged.**

A board packet that cannot show change is just a reprint.

Operators already know the painful version: export, paste, re-color, re-explain. The useful version answers three questions without a scavenger hunt:

1. What are the material risks right now?  
2. What changed since last packet—exposure, assumptions, evidence, owners?  
3. What decision is on the table?

Most demos still show screens. Fewer show a defendable period-over-period path.

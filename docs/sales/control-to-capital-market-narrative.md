**Status:** ACTIVE · canonical GTM claim sheet (2026-07-30)  
**Use for:** offer sheet, Heatmap Amnesty, call KB, IronBoard advice, outbound drafts  
**Do not use:** “most urgent unmet need,” “proves,” “competitors cannot,” “immense void,” “true ALE,” precise cherry-picked CAGRs without one cited dataset

## Headline (publishable)

The next major step in GRC is connecting **continuous compliance evidence** to **business risk**, **financial exposure**, and **portfolio-level decision-making** — across isolated entities.

Not: “The most urgent unmet need in GRC is financially quantified risk management.”

---

## System claim (Ironframe wedge)

**Control-to-Capital GRC**

```
Controls → Evidence → Risks → Business scenarios
  → Estimated financial exposure (ranges)
  → Mitigation cost
  → Residual exposure
```

Runs at:

`Entity → business unit → subsidiary → portfolio company → fund / holding / MSSP command`

with **hard isolation** and **authorized roll-up**.

Competitors already sell pieces (badge automation, FAIR/CRQ modules, workspaces, control crosswalks). Ironframe’s Path B bet is making this **one daily operating loop** — not inventing multi-tenancy or quantification from scratch.

## Gap framing (buyer-safe)

| Gap | Say this | Don’t say this |
|-----|----------|----------------|
| **$ exposure** | Finance-grade buyers need estimated loss exposure (ranges) as the *decision* layer — heatmaps alone aren’t enough for capital allocation. | “Boards are rejecting heatmaps”; “SEC requires FAIR/ALE.” |
| **Multi-entity** | Hub-and-spoke exists elsewhere; our edge is **isolation depth + whole-cent exposure** in one Command seat. | “Nobody builds multi-tenant GRC”; “competitors must rewrite their architecture.” |
| **Frameworks** | Common controls matter; we turn them into **live economic risk context**, not “we uniquely map SOC 2↔NIS2.” | “Existing tools can’t harmonize frameworks.” |

**SEC (accurate):** Item 106 / 1.05 raise accountability for material cyber impact. Materiality includes quantitative *and* qualitative factors — quantification helps defensibility; it is not mandated as FAIR/ALE.

**Heatmaps (accurate):** Keep heatmaps as optional context. Decision layer = scenarios + estimated financial exposure + mitigation cost.

**ALE language:** Prefer **estimated annual loss exposure** / **loss exposure range (e.g. P50–P90)**. Product integrity: **whole-cent / integer-cent** storage — not “true ALE as accounting dollars.”

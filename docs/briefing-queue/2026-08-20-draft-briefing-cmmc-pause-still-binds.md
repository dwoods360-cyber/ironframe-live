> **Executive Summary:** On **13 July 2026**, the Department of War suspended **CMMC Phase II** requirements that had been scheduled to begin **10 November 2026**. That pause is real. It is not a waiver of Phase I self-assessment duties, SPRS scoring where required, NIST SP 800-171 implementation, or **DFARS 252.204-7012** safeguarding and cyber-incident reporting. Mid-August operators who treat "CMMC paused" as "CUI discipline optional" are reading the wrong memo.
>
> *Distinct from the 17 August Unified Agenda desk note (September FAR/CIRCIA cluster). This briefing is about the pause itself. Public governance analysis only — not legal advice and not a product pitch.*

## I. Exposure Vector

Phase II would have pushed broader third-party assessment pathways into the contract clause cadence. Public DoW/DoD communications and subsequent counsel alerts state that Phase II is suspended pending reform review, while **Phase I self-assessment requirements remain firmly in place**. [1][2][3]

Separately, analyses emphasize that **DFARS 252.204-7012** continues to obligate covered defense contractors and subcontractors to safeguard covered defense information and meet rapid cyber-incident reporting duties — independent of the Phase II pause. [2][3]

The operational failure mode in August is therefore not "racing a November Phase II cliff." It is **misreading suspension as relief**: freezing NIST 800-171 remediation, mixing CUI and commercial artifacts in the same evidence store, or assuming SPRS attestations no longer matter because certification theater slowed down.

## II. Quantitative Context

Collapse four tracks into one "CMMC paused" slogan and you lose the clock that still binds.

| Track | Mid-August status (claim hygiene) |
| --- | --- |
| CMMC Phase II (third-party assessment cadence) | Suspended as of 13 July 2026; Nov 2026 transition held [1][2] |
| CMMC Phase I (self-assessment / SPRS where required) | Remains in force per public DoW statements [1][2] |
| DFARS 252.204-7012 | Still live — safeguarding + incident reporting [2][3] |
| Broader FAR / Unified Agenda contracting-cyber cluster | Separate September watch item — see Aug 17 desk note; not this briefing's lead [4] |

The useful quantitative question is not "days until a cancelled Phase II start." It is: **which contracts still flow down 7012 / NIST-aligned duties today**, and **how many days of deferred remediation** accumulate while teams wait for certification theater to restart.

Do not invent whole-cent exposure figures without a documented model. Prefer named clause inventory and residual remediation days with explicit assumptions if you quantify.

## III. What Modern GRC Must Enforce

| Soft habit | Control-system requirement |
| --- | --- |
| "CMMC paused = CUI optional" | Document what Phase II suspension does **not** cancel (Phase I, 7012, NIST 800-171) |
| Frozen POA&Ms during reform review | Keep SPRS integrity and POA&M ownership named while review is underway |
| Shared ticket/GRC store across defense + commercial clients | Prevent soft co-mingling: auditor scoped to one engagement must not retrieve another's pack without a governed export |
| Waiting for November certification theater | Inventory active primes/subs that still flow down 7012 regardless of CMMC assessment status |

### Scope discipline (claim hygiene)

| Claim | Accurate framing |
| --- | --- |
| Phase II pause | Real suspension of the Nov 2026 Phase II transition cadence [1][2] |
| Phase I / SPRS | Remains in force where required — not waived by the pause [1][2] |
| DFARS 252.204-7012 | Still live for covered contractors/subs [2][3] |
| Soft tenancy / shared stores | Assurance failure mode for multi-entity operators — not a claim that every shared GRC tool is unlawful |

### Do not claim

- That Phase II is still mandatorily arriving on 10 November 2026 under the pre-pause schedule
- That the July pause waived DFARS 252.204-7012 or NIST SP 800-171 duties
- That primes are universally "rejecting all SPRS scores" as a proven industry-wide event (needs named, dated evidence if asserted)
- That soft tenancy alone violates a specific CMMC Phase II clause while Phase II is suspended
- Product certifications, feature superiority, or Ironframe as the required fix

## IV. Verification Protocol

1. Re-read the official CMMC About / Phase II suspension notice language: Phase II suspended; Phase I remains. [1]
2. Confirm counsel summaries that 7012 / NIST 800-171 remain despite the pause. [2][3]
3. Keep CIRCIA and September Unified Agenda contracting rules out of the lead claim set (covered elsewhere). [4]
4. If citing Reform Task Force / RFI process, use primary DoW materials and dated comment windows — do not invent a "90-day enforcement crunch."
